Install in a minute

No account, no setup wizard.

Download & drag

Open Clips.dmg and drag Clips into your Applications folder.

Allow pasting

Open Clips and turn it on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, so it can paste for you.

Press ⇧⌘V

That's it. Clips lives in your menu bar. Turn on Launch at Login from its menu.

macOS says it can't verify the developer? Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Clips. You only need to do this once.

Questions

 

Is Clips free?

Yes, Clips is free to download and use.

Does Clips send my data anywhere?

No. Your history is stored only on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Clips. Clips has no account and makes no network requests.

Does it record passwords?

Clips skips anything that password managers (1Password, Bitwarden, Keychain and others) mark as private. You can also pause recording from the menu bar at any time.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

Only to paste the item you pick into the app you're using (it presses ⌘V for you). Without it, Clips still works: picking an item copies it and you press ⌘V yourself.

Can I change the shortcut?

Yes. Click the paperclip in the menu bar → Shortcut, and choose ⇧⌘V, ⌥⌘V, ⌃⌥V or ⇧⌘C.

How many items does it keep?

The last 300 items, plus everything you pin. Pinned items are never removed automatically.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Clips from the menu bar and move it from Applications to the Trash. To also remove your history, delete ~/Library/Application Support/Clips.
